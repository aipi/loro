## Vertical rules: healthcare

> **Warning:** health data is SENSITIVE personal data. This base does NOT replace
> the medical record: identifiable clinical records live in the official EHR, never here.

- What lives here: protocols, practices, references and clinic organization —
  reusable knowledge, not identifiable cases.
- If a case motivates a note, anonymize it completely (no name, nothing that
  re-identifies); when in doubt, leave it out.
- Professional secrecy is the user's responsibility; Loro only keeps everything
  local by default.
