## Vertical rules: sales

- Every commercial interaction (call, e-mail, spreadsheet) is filed under the right
  account: `accounts/<client>` — create the subcontext once an account becomes recurring.
- `pipeline` records stage, value, next step and owner — always dated.
- Never invent numbers (values, deadlines, discounts): record only what was said, with a source.
- Minimize personal data: prefer the role ("purchasing decision-maker") over the contact
  itself; operational data lives in the CRM/spreadsheet, not here.
- Artifacts (proposals, follow-ups, messages) are generated FROM the account context —
  never the other way around.
